# Weapon roster — shared look and short briefs

Supporting brief for Fable, based on `CLAUDE.md` section 9 at look-pass commit `45cf8948974623d8d8165a4cf9c0377e2cb619f2`. Every roster role is mapped below using a descriptive label rather than the source's real-world product names or game references. These are original design proposals, not new official item names or authorization to expand Phase 1. Build/refine the rifle and test pistol first; the rest is a visual vocabulary for later approved content. No Unity execution, gameplay validation or Deck profiling was performed.

The core rifle and test-only pistol are detailed in [rifle-and-pistol-notes.md](rifle-and-pistol-notes.md); this document covers the other section 9 gun/melee roles, its throwable rock and its exempt rope tool. The oversized handgun below is a future roster weapon, distinct from the test pistol. The final loadout stays **one gun + one melee secondary**, with the rope tool exempt. Unlimited ammunition does not remove the current magazine/reload readability requirement. No new firing modes, damage values, powers or loadout slots are implied by an art brief.

## One workshop, different functions

Make every weapon feel like it could be serviced aboard the starter lander: rounded load-bearing shells over dark mechanisms, broad glove controls, cool painted housings, a warm tactile grip and one repair. Share a fastener family, bevel widths and control scale; do not reuse one box body at different lengths. The owner is unaligned. If faction variations are later requested, call them only **Faction A, B, C** and use panel shapes/value patterns in addition to colour; do not assign this whole roster to a faction or invent insignia.

- **Primary shape:** one clear mass per function — fork, stub body, huge upper, long precision tube, quiet small tube, drum, offset chamber, twin bores. At 20 m/1280×800 the class must survive a black silhouette; fine differences between two tiny pistols cannot be guaranteed. Use the source brief's FOV/projection check, not a demand for readable mechanics in six pixels.
- **Proportions:** grips and controls fit the same space-suit glove as the first two guns. Start with 45–55 mm grip width, 120–140 mm usable grip length, a clear 40×55 mm trigger opening and large 22–30 mm paddles. Fit actual glove geometry before freezing dimensions. Small weapons shorten their body, never the player's hand.
- **Panel geometry:** one major inset cover, one overlap collar and a few big fasteners. Use real recesses/lips; no black rectangles pretending to be vents, grip textures or ports. Avoid uniformly tiled screws and narrow rails. Every added feature must affect silhouette, plane change, material separation or handling story.
- **Bevels:** main gun housings 4–8 mm, small pistol parts 2–4 mm; 1–3 segments where they earn a visible curve. Long close-view rounds need a continuous outline. Keep exported split normals coherent across curves, with intentional hard edges on folds. Do not use flat shading on every triangle or soften every mechanical joint into melted rubber.
- **Wear:** 2–4% of visible painted surface at heel, latch travel, carry contact and servicing edges; heat colour only near a hot outlet. Hand-authored broad patches beat random all-edge brightness. No painted directional shine or dense grey grime. Big quiet surfaces must remain between repairs.
- **Crosshair:** start with the rifle brief's 96 px radius exclusion circle around screen centre at native Deck resolution. Keep permanent fins, forks, horns, hoses and glow strips outside it at rest/kick. Judge attack/reload transient coverage separately. No enormous weapon shell blocking threats to justify a “powerful” silhouette.

A rare variant must retain class silhouette and controls, with no whole-weapon bloom or enlarged silhouette that changes combat identification.

### Pipeline and budgets

Blender work is scripted in `tools/blender/`; `oa_blender` exports `OAM1` geometry `.bytes` with named parts, positions, split normals, RGB8 sRGB and position sockets. No textures, UVs, normal maps or decal sheets. The exporter maps Blender +Y forward/+Z up into game +Z forward/+Y up and reverses triangle winding for that axis swap. Keep the origin at the firing hand; do not rotate the meshes a second time on import. `ModelLibrary` uses exported normals, converts sRGB colours to linear in a linear project, and makes one renderer per named part. The current paint helper uses a POINT colour attribute: split vertices/source objects at hard colour boundaries.

For the current gun path, use exact socket names `Muzzle`, `SupportHand`, `Magazine`; factory creates `Grip` at origin and recognizes the exact separate part `Magazine` for reload hiding. These are position sockets, not a rig or arbitrary articulated pivots. A revolver cylinder, shotgun hinge, moving fork, folding blade or melee attack is **not** automatically supported by this loader. Give the implementer a parts/pivots proposal; do not name an unrelated mechanism `Magazine` just to make it disappear. Existing prefab custom sockets follow a different bounds-centre convention.

Proposed near-view budgets below are triangulated mesh targets including bevels, caps, back faces and separate components. They are not measured costs or existing asset counts. Start simple, allocate detail to the nearest surfaces and minimize renderer count within real animation needs. For future world/dropped variants aim at roughly 25% of the near triangle budget, with 300–600 triangle far versions for larger tools; LOD switching needs explicit support later. Never multiply 8,000-triangle hero meshes across a large enemy crowd without that plan. No new meshes are made in this task.

### Unspecified “silly space guns” and “other goofy items”

Section 9 intentionally leaves these open; the following are **optional visual pitches, not committed roster additions or gameplay abilities**. Pick at most one after the core look is accepted. Humour should arise from a practical object in an unlikely role, not a copied joke weapon or a layer of random objects.

- **Pressure-bell projector:** a squat instrument bell on a rounded pump body, one flattened carry handle, warm padded grip and cream wide mouth. The large flare gives a silhouette distinct from the gravity fork; keep its upper lip out of the aim circle. Proposed 3,500 triangles. No firing effect is specified; reject a household appliance replica or an all-glowing mouth.
- **Sample-capsule launcher:** three broad sample-capsule bulges inside a low rectangular cradle, one wide rear latch, slate body/cream pods/ochre control. Proposed 4,000 triangles. Keep each bulge large and structural; no tiny glass tubes or implied damage/status effects. Reject similarity to an existing game's launcher and avoid transparent multi-layer capsule shells.
- **Radiator paddle melee prop:** a broad rounded sheet with three thick shallow ribs on a short insulated handle, one visibly replaced corner. Warm grey sheet `#9B9B8C`, slate ribs, warm grip, proposed 1,600 triangles. It must read as maintainable ship equipment; no sharp fin forest and no new electrical power implied.

## Roster acceptance sheet

Request one same-scale black silhouette contact sheet of every approved weapon, then native 1280×800 images in the actual player pose with glove and crosshair, on an enemy at 20 m, and dropped against light and dark ground. Repeat daylight, low Sun and lamp-lit night; include magazine-hidden or other implemented mechanism states. Blender previews help construction critique but cannot establish Unity framing, shader response or Deck cost.

Ask for yes/no per item: distinct class mass? common glove scale? aim circle clear at rest/kick? meaningful broad bevels? primary palette separable in grayscale? sparse plausible wear? all sides capped for a dropped view? correct exported socket/part names? no copied profile, logos or faction names? counts and renderers recorded? Any no blocks visual sign-off. Candidate pitches remain deferred until chosen; image generation, model creation, mechanics implementation and actual captures are outside this brief.
